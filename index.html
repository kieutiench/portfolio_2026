How I run a project

I believe in keeping things simple: set clear goals, make a practical plan, communicate openly, and get things done.

  1. phase_01

    Initiate

    Gather goals with clients and decision‑makers, confirm expectations and agree what success looks like.

  2. phase_02

    Plan

    Break the work into milestones and owners, set the timeline and agree the reporting rhythm.

  3. phase_03

    Execute

    Coordinate teams across functions, track milestones and flag gaps early.

  4. phase_04

    Close

    Wrap up deliverables, review results against KPIs and capture lessons for the next project.
